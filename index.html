A jam, your way

Making music,
without strict rules.

Maybe you've been playing for years. Maybe you're picking up an instrument for the first time. Maybe you'd rather just listen for a while. All of that is welcome.

01

Predictable & relaxed

We'll check in beforehand about what feels comfortable. Take a break, arrive late or leave early — it's all okay.

02

No experience needed

You don't need to know songs or read music. A sound, a rhythm or a listening ear is more than enough.

03

There is room for everyone

Autistic, anxious, shy, neurotypical or something else entirely: we make space for different ways of taking part.
